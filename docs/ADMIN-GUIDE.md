# Admin Guide (for hotel staff)

Sign in at **/admin** with the account your Super Admin created. The menu shows only the areas your role allows.

## Daily operations
- **Dashboard:** today's arrivals and departures, occupancy, pending reservations, revenue (managers only), recent payments and activity. Switch between Today, 7 days, 30 days, 12 months or a custom range.
- **Reservations:** search by reference, name, email or phone, and filter by status, payment, room type and dates. Open a reservation to:
  - **Confirm** or **reject** pending requests.
  - **Check in** and **check out** (an early check-out frees the remaining nights).
  - **Assign or change rooms**. Occupied rooms can't be picked.
  - **Modify dates or guests**. Availability is re-checked and the stay re-priced.
  - **Record a payment** taken at the desk (cash, card, UPI, bank transfer).
  - **Cancel**. The policy penalty is calculated automatically; managers can waive it.
  - **Issue refunds** (managers). Refund status is shown separately.
  - **Add internal notes** and **print an invoice**.
- **Calendar:** room-by-room view of who is in which room, and when. Click a bar to open the reservation.
- **New reservation:** for phone, email and walk-in guests.
- **Inquiries:** contact, wedding, dining and spa requests. Assign them, add notes, reply by email and mark them resolved.

## Rooms & revenue
- **Room types:** photos (drag in high-resolution images), description, occupancy, bed, size, amenities, base and weekend price, extra-guest and bed charges, minimum and maximum stay, featured flag, and publish status.
- **Inventory:** physical rooms (numbers, housekeeping status, out of service), maintenance blocks, stop-sell (blackout) dates, and a 14-day availability grid.
- **Pricing & taxes:**
  - Seasonal rates: overrides or % and ₹ adjustments by date, weekday and room.
  - Holiday uplifts, meal plans and add-ons.
  - Taxes and fees: GST slabs are preconfigured.
  - The price preview checks what a guest would pay.
- **Offers & coupons:** packages shown on the website. Discounting offers apply automatically to eligible stays. Coupon codes can have limits and validity dates.
- **Reports:** bookings, revenue, occupancy, room performance (ADR, RevPAR), cancellations, offers and coupons, and booking sources. Export to CSV or print.

## Website (no code needed)
- **Theme & site:**
  - Choose a design preset, or set your own colours, fonts, corner style, button style and spacing.
  - Upload the logo and favicon, and edit the menu, footer, announcement bar, contact details, WhatsApp, social links, SEO and Google Analytics.
  - Work is saved as a **draft** and shown in the live preview on the right. Press **Publish** to go live. **History** restores earlier designs.
- **Pages:** every page is a stack of sections (hero, text, rooms, offers, gallery, testimonials, FAQ, contact form, map, 3D tour and more). You can:
  - add, hide, reorder, duplicate or remove sections
  - edit text and images
  - reset a section to its default content
  - preview on desktop, tablet or mobile
  - publish now or schedule publishing
  - restore earlier revisions
  - create new pages, which you then link from the menu in Theme & site
- **Media library:** upload images (optimised automatically). Always add alt text describing the image; it matters for accessibility and SEO.
- **Gallery, Journal, FAQs:** manage the photo gallery, blog articles and travel guides, and the FAQ page.
- **Reviews:** approve or reject guest reviews, feature the best ones on the home page, write public responses, and add press quotes.
- **Newsletter:** see subscribers and export them for your email tool.

## Administration (Super Admin)
- **Staff & roles:** create accounts, change roles, deactivate leavers (signed out immediately) and reset passwords. Adjust what each role may do.
- **Settings:**
  - check-in/out times, weekend nights, booking window, room-hold time
  - online payment and pay-at-hotel, deposit %
  - auto-confirmation, cancellation policy, staff notification emails
  - integration status and the email outbox
- **Audit log:** every important change, with who made it and when.

### Roles at a glance
| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Super Admin | Everything, including staff, roles and settings |
| Hotel Manager | Everything except staff management and system settings; refunds and financial reports included |
| Booking Manager | Reservations, inventory, pricing, offers, customers, operational reports |
| Receptionist | Reservations (check-in/out, rooms, notes, desk payments), customers, inquiries |
| Content Editor | Pages, theme, media, gallery, journal, FAQs, reviews, newsletter |
